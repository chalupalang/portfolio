About

Hi, I'm Kaylah Lang.

I'm a student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, double majoring in Supply Chain Management and Marketing, with a collateral in International Business and a minor in Chinese. I began photography when I bought my first and only DSLR on March 2nd, 2024, and started out photographing my cat, Chalupa. From there I gradually branched into shooting the outside world, and eventually people.

Now I bring my camera everywhere I travel. I've photographed in the United States, Mexico, and China. I've discovered that no matter the city, I always end up photographing the stray and pet cats I come across. It's become my favorite subject, and a bit of a personal tradition.
